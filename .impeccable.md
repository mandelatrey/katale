## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience across three contexts:
- **Farmers in the field** — checking prices on mobile before deciding where to sell, often on slow connections
- **Traders & brokers** — making bulk buying/selling decisions on desktop/tablet, tracking price trends
- **Agribusiness staff** — professional operations users (e.g., brokers like Ismail M.) on desktop

The interface must work across all three: information-dense on desktop, fast and scannable on mobile.

### Brand Personality
Modern, professional, confident.

Agribridge is a serious agritech/fintech product. It should signal credibility to operators and investors while remaining accessible to field users. No decoration for decoration's sake. Data is the product.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean and data-focused — like Bloomberg Terminal meets Notion. Every pixel earns its place.
- Information density is a feature, not a bug
- Existing dark green sidebar (#0d3b1a) with light content area is the right structural direction
- Primary brand color: deep agricultural green (#1f8a3e)
- Typography should feel editorial and precise, not friendly/rounded
- Maps and charts are first-class citizens — UI exists to frame them, not compete with them

Anti-references: Avoid generic SaaS dashboards (purple-to-blue gradients, glassmorphism, "AI vibes"). Avoid overly decorative agricultural themes (leaf icons on every heading, earthy textures as backgrounds).

### Design Principles
1. **Data over decoration** — charts, prices, and maps are the UI; chrome should recede
2. **Density with clarity** — compact layouts for power users, but scannable hierarchy for first-timers
3. **Confident restraint** — the dark sidebar and green palette are strong enough; resist adding more visual weight
4. **Context-aware** — desktop can show more simultaneously; mobile must prioritize the single most useful action
5. **Trust through consistency** — professional users notice inconsistency; every spacing, color, and label decision must be deliberate
